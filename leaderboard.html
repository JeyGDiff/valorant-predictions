What is Elo? A rating system originally from chess. Everyone starts at 1500 and the number moves after every series — the higher the number, the stronger the team's track record. Cumulative ratings are built from the full VCT main-event history; only teams active in the last 90 days appear, minimum 10 matches.
Player Elo works the same way but is attached to individual players, so when a star transfers their rating follows them. A team's strength in the model is the average Elo of its five rostered players, making it substitution-aware. Cumulative ratings cover the full VCT main-event history; only players active in the last 90 days appear, minimum 10 matches.
Season view: Elo resets to 1500 at the start of each year — rankings reflect only that year's performance. Ratings are confidence-weighted, so small hot streaks don't inflate the board. Minimum 5 VCT-main matches to appear.
