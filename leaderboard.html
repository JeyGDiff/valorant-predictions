What is Elo? It's a rating system originally from chess. Beat a stronger team and your rating jumps; lose to a weaker one and it drops. Everyone starts at 1500 and the number moves after every series. The higher the number, the stronger the team's track record. Cumulative ratings are built from the full VCT main-event match history — only teams active in the last 90 days appear here. Minimum 10 matches to appear.
Player Elo works the same way but is attached to individual players, not team logos — so when a star player transfers, their rating follows them. A team's strength in the model is the average Elo of its five rostered players, making this substitution-aware: a roster swap immediately changes the team's predicted strength. Season view: Elo resets to 1500 at the start of each year — rankings reflect only that year's performance. Win rate and match count are within the year only. Ratings are confidence-weighted: a player with 17 matches counts for less than one with 50, so small hot streaks don't inflate the leaderboard. Minimum 5 VCT-main matches to appear.
