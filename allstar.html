Optimal Lineup Search

All-Star Roster

The strongest five-player lineup the model can assemble — globally and by region. Every lineup is scored against the same baseline opponent so win probabilities are comparable across rosters.

How this works: We took every active player (competed in the last 90 days, at least 10 career matches) and asked the model: which 5-player combination would beat a typical VCT-level team by the most? Every lineup is scored against the same baseline opponent — the average of all active players worldwide — so win probabilities are comparable across rosters and regions. The global search picks from the top 30 active players by Elo, evaluating all 142,506 possible lineups. Regional searches do the same within each league (Americas · EMEA · Pacific · China), using the top 15 from that region while still scoring against the global average opponent.

Select region

Methodology note: Win probability is scored against a fixed "average opponent" built from the mean stats of all active players — a relative measure, not a literal win rate in a live tournament. Player ratings use the same Elo system that powers the leaderboard.

These rosters are purely stat-driven and not role-aware. The model doesn't know a real team needs an IGL, a duelist, a sentinel, and so on — it just finds the five players whose combined stats predict the highest win probability. Read it as "who are the five best individual performers right now," not "who would make the most functional team."
