How the numbers work

Each team carries a per-map profile built from its match history — overall win rate, how often it picks the map, and its round win rate on attack vs defense. A map's win chance starts from the gap between the two teams' map win rates, nudged by a small pick advantage and the better starting side. The optimal path is a minimax search over the whole veto: on your turns it maximizes your series win probability, on the opponent's turns it minimizes it. These are descriptive heuristics over history, not the win-probability model used on the Predictions page.
