How it works

The model looks at how each of the 10 players have been performing recently, then asks: based on current form, which roster is stronger? It was trained on ~32,000 professional matches scraped from vlr.gg going back to 2020.

Under the hood: a gradient-boosted classifier (XGBoost) with 587 features per match. Each player is described by rolling averages across their last 5, 10, and 20 matches plus career — covering ACS, KDA, KAST, first-blood rate, clutch rate, and more. Match features are built by aggregating each roster and differencing the two sides, so the model sees how much better one team is, not just raw numbers. A dual Elo system (team-level + player-level, substitution-aware) and pairwise roster chemistry features (shared games and win rate for every player pair) are layered on top.

The track record is honest. Every past prediction used only data from before that match. The model trained on the oldest 80% of history, then predicted the remaining 20% in 200-match blocks, retraining after each — the same way it operates live. No look-ahead bias.

Predictions refresh every 6 hours via GitHub Actions. The model and training data are not publicly distributed.

These predictions are for entertainment and analytical purposes only. Do not use them to inform betting decisions. No model is reliable enough to beat the house, and past accuracy does not guarantee future results.
