Each player becomes a vector of point-in-time form — rolling averages over their last 5, 10, 20 matches and full career. Built only from matches before this one.

Each feature is pooled across the five players — not just the mean, but the min, max and spread, so depth and star-power both register.

how it’s validated — no look-ahead

The model trains on the oldest matches, then predicts the next quarter before ever seeing it, folds those results in, refits, and moves forward — the same way it runs live. Every bar below was forecast on a model that had only seen the past.
